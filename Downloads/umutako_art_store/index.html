Our Story

Celebrating Rwanda's
Artistic Heritage

Umutako Art Store was founded with a single mission: to connect the world with the extraordinary talent of Rwandan artisans. The name Umutako means "gift" in Kinyarwanda — and every piece we sell truly is one.

We work directly with over 40 artisan cooperatives across Rwanda, ensuring fair wages, sustainable materials, and cultural preservation with every sale.
